THIRD-PARTY NOTICES
===================

Blitzkrieg ships binaries owned by other people. This file carries the notices
their licences require to travel with those copies. It is maintained by hand
and reviewed like source: nothing extracts it from an upstream file at build
time, because a text scrape that silently stops matching would quietly drop a
notice we are obliged to carry.

When the pinned version of a bundled program changes in build.zig.zon, update
the version recorded below and re-check the notice against that release.
