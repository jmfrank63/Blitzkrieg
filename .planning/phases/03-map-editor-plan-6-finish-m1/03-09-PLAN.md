<objective>
Object pictures in the palette (D-29; carried from plan 5).

Purpose and the finding this plan is built around: D-29 was decided after research said the object database has no icon and an icon "must be a rendered thumbnail". The shipped data says otherwise: every object's folder may hold `icon.tga` - `git ls-tree -r HEAD Data` counts 1125 of them - and the MFC editor's palette showed exactly those (`MapEditor/TabSimpleObjectsDialog.cpp:716-760`, decoded with `IImageProcessor::LoadImage` and scaled with `CreateScaleBySize`); the spec's catalogue record also lists "icon". Task 1 builds that path end to end through the engine (on demand per group, cached) and measures how many placeable objects have a picture; Task 2 asks Johannes, with that number, how to treat the rest - including the engine-render route D-29 names; Task 3 finishes the chosen route.
Output: `BkEditorObjectPicture`, `pictures.zig`, pictures in the palette, a recorded decision.
</objective>
