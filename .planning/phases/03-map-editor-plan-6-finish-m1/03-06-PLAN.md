<objective>
Camera rotation (D-12: free 360 degree yaw at the game's tilt; Alt+Q/E and the trackpad rotate gesture; plain Q/E keep turning the selected object) - measured before it is built.

Purpose and the risk this plan exists for: research took rotation to be "extend SetPlacement's fYaw" (03-RESEARCH.md). Reading the renderer says otherwise: `CTerrain::MovePatches` (`Scene/TerrainInternal.cpp:237-256`) lays the terrain's patches out on a fixed isometric screen grid - only the map corner's screen position comes from the view matrix - and its tiles are pre-transformed screen quads; buildings and infantry are `SGVOT_SPRITE` pictures drawn from one direction (`Main/GameDB.h:13-19`). The session's own comment (`EditorBridge/session.cpp:1321-1327`) records that a camera at the wrong yaw left the ground thousands of pixels away. So at any yaw but the game's the terrain probably does not follow the camera, and "seeing behind buildings" is not in the sprites. The project's rule is to measure graphics, not guess (memory "Measure graphics artefacts"), so Task 1 wires yaw into the bridge and measures, Task 2 puts the numbers in front of Johannes, and Task 3 builds the input only if the renderer draws every yaw correctly.
Output: `BkEditorSetYaw`, a measured table, a recorded decision, and (if chosen) the rotation input.
</objective>
