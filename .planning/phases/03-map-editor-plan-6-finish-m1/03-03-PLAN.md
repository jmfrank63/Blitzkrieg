<objective>
The spec's safe save (D-19): write a temporary file, have the bridge read it back and compare it with what it meant to write, keep one `name.bzm.bak` taken at the session's first write to that file, and swap the temporary file in only when all of that succeeded. Today `NMapFile::Write` writes straight into the destination (`MapFile.cpp:148-196`) and `Editor.save` hands it the real path.

Purpose: the editor becomes safe to rely on - a failed save, a crash mid-write or an autosave (D-20, 03-07) can never destroy the last good map.
Output: `core/files.zig`, `Editor.save` rewritten, `BkEditorSaveMap` verifying its own write, tests on a fake and on a real disk.
</objective>
