<objective>
Camera zoom like the game (D-10..D-16): the game's zoom is not a camera distance but NSceneScreenScale's global-driven orthographic rescale (`Scene/SceneScreenScale.h`), bounded by `GetMaxZoomSteps` and read from `GFX.World.BaseSizeX/Y`, which only the game's Mission screen publishes. The bridge publishes the base itself, applies `CInterfaceMission::ApplyZoomStep`'s pointer-anchored recipe (`GameTT/iMissionInternal.cpp:881-905`), and reports the view; the app zooms on Shift+wheel/swipe and pinch, resets on Home, pans 1:1 at any zoom and remembers each map's view for the session. With `BkEditorWorldToScreen` the brush outline (carried from plan 5) is drawn on the terrain.

Purpose: the M1 scope's "camera scroll, rotate and zoom" - zoom half; rotation is 03-06.
Output: five bridge entry points, the app's zoom input and view memory, the brush outline, engine-tier and smoke checks.
</objective>
