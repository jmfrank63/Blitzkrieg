# Glossary: Hindi (हिन्दी)

Scope: terms of the main menu, the mission briefing, unit types and ranks, so that the overlays (S02 T04), the checker (S03) and the documents (S08) use one translation per term. The English column is the 2003 game's wording.

Review state: the menu terms (the thirteen main-menu labels and the header) are the S01 overlay texts in `Localization/game/hi/`; the unit and rank terms are a machine seed that still needs native review.
