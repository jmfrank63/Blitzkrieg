# Glossary: Japanese (日本語)

Scope: terms of the main menu, the mission briefing, unit types and ranks, so that the overlays (S02 T04), the checker (S03) and the documents (S08) use one translation per term. The English column is the 2003 game's wording.

Style rules (M003 D100): no ASCII space between Japanese words; fullwidth （）「」 for brackets and quotes; no ― or ～ in boxed texts (the engine's no-start set holds U+2025, U+2026, U+30A0 and U+FF5E, but a dash also reads as a break in the box).

Unit naming: German Panzer by Roman numeral and 号 (IV号戦車, III号突撃砲, ASCII numerals I V X); the names Tiger and Panther as ティーガー and パンター; Sd.Kfz. stays in Latin; FlaK stays Latin and the gun type follows as 高射砲 (FlaK18 88mm高射砲); British and American tanks by katakana name with the type first (M4シャーマン, クロムウェル, チャーチル); Soviet T-34, KV-1 and IS-2 keep their designations, the rocket launcher is カチューシャ.

Ranks beyond the table (Wehrmacht and Red Army, as the database uses them; the nine glossaries keep equal row counts, so these are notes, not rows): 一等兵 private first class, 上等兵 lance corporal, 曹長 senior sergeant, 少尉 second lieutenant, 中佐 lieutenant colonel, 少将 major general, 中将 lieutenant general, 元帥 field marshal (ソ連邦元帥 for the Marshal of the Soviet Union).

Review state: the menu terms (the thirteen main-menu labels and the header) are the S01 overlay texts in `Localization/game/ja/`; the unit and rank terms are a machine seed that still needs native review. The options and tutorial labels (`最適化オプション`, `直感操作ガイド`) stay as they are: they are the regional Han form probes of `l10n-game-reads-it` (化 and 直), see `Localization/README.md`.
